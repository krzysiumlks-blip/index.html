1. About the app

Prism Burst is a single-player puzzle game for Android. You can play offline. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

2. Data we process

Stored only on your device (not sent to our servers):

  • Game settings (for example effects on/off, color palette)
  • High scores, daily progress, level and adventure progress
  • Other local gameplay state needed to run the game

We do not require an account. We do not operate our own game servers that receive your personal data for the core game.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone data for the core game.

3. Advertising and analytics

Version 1.0: no ads and no third-party analytics SDKs.

If a future version includes advertising (for example Google AdMob) or analytics, third-party services may process device identifiers and ad-related data under their own policies. This Privacy Policy will be updated before such features are added.

4. Children

Prism Burst is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a future feature, contact us at the email above.

5. Data retention

Local game data stays on your device until you clear the app’s storage in Android settings, uninstall the app, or use an in-app reset where available.

6. Your choices

  • Clear app storage in Android system settings to remove local progress
  • Uninstall the app
  • Contact us with privacy questions: prismanovacreatio@gmail.com

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. The current version will always be available at this page URL.
